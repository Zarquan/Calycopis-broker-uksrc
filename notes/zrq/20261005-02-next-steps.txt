    Compute resource sessions keep their +ve sizes
    Compute allocation allocations have +ve sizes
    JOIN query subtracts sum(sessions) from sum(allocations) to calculate the available space.
    that makes the query more complicated, but the entities are simpler to handle.

        Existing entity type.

            class:  DockerSimpleComputeResourceEntity
            table:  dockersimplecomputeresources
            fields:
                - minrequestedcores
                - maxrequestedcores
                - minofferedcores
                - maxofferedcores
                - minrequestedmemory
                - maxrequestedmemory
                - minofferedmemory
                - maxofferedmemory

        New entity type.

            class:  DockerSimpleComputeAllocationEntity
            table:  dockersimplecomputeallocations
            fields:
                - allocationstart
                - allocationend
                - allocatedcores
                - allocatedmemory

    Lithosia-container defaults to /workspace, mounted as a volume

    DSH_HOME points to /dsh

    README says launch with one or two volumes

        --env DSH_WORKSPACE=/workspace
        --volume $(pwd):/workspace

        --env DSH_HOME=/dsh
        --volume ${DHS_HOME:?}:/dsh

    Or you can place multiple projects into /workspace

        --volume ${HOME}/projects/project-A:/workspace/project-A

        --volume ${HOME}/projects/project-B:/workspace/project-B

    You can make things portable by adding environment variables

        --env PROJECT_A=/workspace/project-A
        --volume ${HOME}/projects/project-A:/workspace/project-A

        --env PROJECT_B=/workspace/project-B
        --volume ${HOME}/projects/project-B:/workspace/project-B
